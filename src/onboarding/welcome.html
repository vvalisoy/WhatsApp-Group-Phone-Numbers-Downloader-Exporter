WA Group Number Exporter is installed

Export the phone numbers of any WA Web group you're in to Excel, CSV or a vCard (VCF) contacts file, in under a minute.

First

Pin the extension so it's one click away: click the puzzle icon 🧩 in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to WA Group Number Exporter.

Export a group in 4 steps

  1. Open WA Web and the group

    Go to web.whatsapp.com, sign in, and open the group chat you want to export.

Full mode: get saved contacts' numbers too

In the "View all" list, people saved in your phone appear by name only, so normal mode can't see their numbers. Turn on Full mode in the extension, open the group chat (no "View all" needed) and press Start. It reads the member list WA Web already has loaded and finishes in about a second.

Full mode depends on WA Web internals. If it says "Full mode unavailable", use normal mode.

Free and open source

No limits, no account and no payments. Every export includes all members, in every format. The source code is public, so anyone can check exactly what the extension does.

Using your exported file

Excel

Use Export → Excel and open the .xlsx file directly. Numbers keep their leading + and the header row has filters.

Google Sheets

File → Import → Upload → choose the CSV. Untick "Convert text to numbers, dates and formulas" to keep the +.

Google Contacts

contacts.google.com → Import → select the .vcf file. Contacts sync to your Android phone.

iPhone

AirDrop or email the .vcf to yourself and open it on the iPhone → Add All Contacts. Or import at icloud.com/contacts.

Troubleshooting

"Open the group's View all members list first"

The extension couldn't find the members list. Open Group info → View all, then press Start again. If the group has no "View all" button, turn on Full mode.

Some members have no phone number

In the "View all" list, contacts saved in your phone show only a name. Use Full mode to get those numbers. WA Web also hides some members' numbers for privacy (for example in community announcement groups). No tool can get those numbers.

Some members have no name

That member hasn't set a WA name. They're exported with their number and an empty name. In the VCF their contact name is the phone number.

It stopped early or the count looks too low

Keep the WA Web tab in front and the members list open until it says Done. Then press Start again. Results are de-duplicated.

Nothing works after a WA Web update

Click Diagnose in the extension and attach the report to a GitHub issue. It contains only page structure, with no names or numbers.

Your data stays on your computer

  • Numbers are read from the WA Web page you have open and saved only in your browser.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The extension makes no network requests at all: no servers, no account, no tracking.
  • Clear in the extension deletes the collected list.

Use exported numbers responsibly. Group members' numbers are personal data, and messaging people who didn't agree to it is spam and may get your own account restricted.
